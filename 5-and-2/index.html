Sourdough

5 & 2

5 & 2 Co. Sourdough is a Swiss Pantry brand. The bread is hand mixed and long fermented.

House-made sourdough is on the deli board: the Cajun turkey, ham and Swiss, the BLT, chicken salad, and grilled cheese. Swedish rye, whole wheat, and the sub buns are house-made on the sandwiches that name them.

Call (931) 962-0567 to ask which loaves are out that day.
