Bakery

Guests come for bread, cookies, cakes, donuts, pies, and pastries. Prices for the bakery case are at the counter. This page lists what the bakery has published, and it leaves the rest for a conversation at the store.

Donut Saturday

The first and third Saturday of the month, from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. On those Saturdays the store opens an hour earlier than a regular Saturday.

Next Donut Saturday is Saturday, November 7, 2026.

The one after that is Saturday, November 21, 2026.

The usual glazes are vanilla, maple, and chocolate, and each month has a specialty donut. The recipes have been the same for over 20 years. Call (931) 962-0567 if you want to know what is still in the case.

Fried pies

Fried pies are made on Mondays and Thursdays.

  • Peach
  • Apricot
  • Apple
  • Blueberry
  • Raspberry
  • Blackberry
  • Triple berry
  • Chocolate
  • Pecan
  • Coconut

Strawberry pies

Fresh strawberry pies are a yearly favorite from March through the end of May.

Holiday fruitcake

Fruitcake is in the shop from October through December. It can be shipped, or picked up at the store.
