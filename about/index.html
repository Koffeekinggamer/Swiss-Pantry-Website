The Masseys

About

Luke and Victoria Massey bought the bakery in April 2025. The Swiss Pantry already had about 35 years of tradition in Belvidere when they took it on.

The store is still a bakery, a deli, and a place to stock a pantry. 5 & 2 Co. Sourdough is the sourdough brand inside that same shop: hand mixed and long fermented, and baked for the counter and the sandwich board.

Guests who have been coming for years still talk about the donuts, the fried pies, and a staff that knows the regulars. The hours, Donut Saturday, and the published sandwich menu are the practical parts of keeping that welcome clear.
