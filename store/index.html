The Store

The Swiss Pantry is a bakery and a bulk-food store on the same floor. Guests stock up on pantry staples, candies, casseroles, and everyday goods, including Amish and Mennonite products. Online orders will be placed through Square.

Shop online

Bulk foods

The shelves hold bulk foods, candies, deli meats, and cheeses, along with gluten-free and sugar-free items in the bulk selection. Gift baskets can be made on request. Call (931) 962-0567 and ask.

Triple T Farms

Beef comes from Triple T Farms: fresh, pasture-raised ground beef, with special cuts by pre-order. Mild and hot sausage is made with fresh pork sourced by Triple T Farms.

Soups

Homemade soups are seasonal, in the fall and winter. Ask which soup is on that day.
